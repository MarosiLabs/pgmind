# move-edit

A block whose hash is gone *and* whose position is gone. RFC-004 Part B names this an open question outright: stage 1's alignment is order-monotonic, so an edited block that also crossed another block is structurally invisible to it. These cases separate "moved" from "moved and edited" so the published number says which half is hard.

## case: move-unedited

**why:** control — a pure reorder with no edits. Exact content match carries all of it with no heuristic involved, and a rebinder that scores badly *here* is broken rather than merely imprecise.

### before

`````markdown
# Doc

Alpha paragraph.

Beta paragraph.

Gamma paragraph.
`````

### after

`````markdown
# Doc

Gamma paragraph.

Alpha paragraph.

Beta paragraph.
`````

## case: move-edit-cross-section

**why:** one paragraph moves to another section and is edited in the same write; a new paragraph takes its old position, which is exactly the bait for a positional aligner.

### before

`````markdown
## Alpha

The reconciler re-parents before it removes, so a carried child never dangles.

## Beta

Beta has its own paragraph.
`````

### after

`````markdown
## Alpha

Alpha has lost its paragraph.

## Beta

Beta has its own paragraph.

The reconciler re-parents before it deletes, so a carried child never dangles.
`````

## case: swap-and-edit

**why:** two blocks swap *and* both are edited. Any binding here is a crossing match, so an order-monotonic aligner cannot make both — it must choose one and drop the other, or drop both.

### before

`````markdown
First paragraph, about tiles and their fixed size.

Second paragraph, about blocks and their spans.
`````

### after

`````markdown
Second paragraph, about blocks and their byte spans.

First paragraph, about tiles and their fixed sizes.
`````

## case: section-move-with-inner-edit

**why:** a whole section moves and one paragraph inside it changes. The unedited half carries deterministically; the edited half is the measurement.

### before

`````markdown
## One

Paragraph in section one that stays exactly as written.

## Two

Paragraph in section two that will be edited during the move.
`````

### after

`````markdown
## Two

Paragraph in section two that was edited during the move.

## One

Paragraph in section one that stays exactly as written.
`````

## case: move-edit-one-word

**why:** the last block moves to the front with a single word changed. Similarity is overwhelming and position is maximally wrong — the cleanest single measurement of whether identity survives a move.

### before

`````markdown
Alpha.

Beta.

Gamma.

Delta.

The excision log records what was removed and never the removed bytes.
`````

### after

`````markdown
The excision log records what was erased and never the removed bytes.

Alpha.

Beta.

Gamma.

Delta.
`````
