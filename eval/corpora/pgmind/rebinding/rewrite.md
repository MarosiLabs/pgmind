# rewrite

Whole-document replacement where most or all of the prose is new. This is the **precision** half of the corpus: the correct answer is often "carry nothing", and an aligner that leans on position scores near-perfect recall here while being wrong about every block.

## case: rewrite-total

**why:** identical structure, no shared content. Every binding a rebinder makes here is a false one, and history, citations and embeddings would follow each of them onto text that is not theirs.

### before

`````markdown
# Storage

Tiles hold bytes in fixed-size runs.

Blocks hold meaning under a stable name.
`````

### after

`````markdown
# Layout

Fragments keep raw text inside bounded chunks.

Units keep semantics with a durable identifier.
`````

- (nothing carries)

## case: rewrite-keep-one

**why:** one paragraph survives verbatim inside a document that is otherwise entirely new. Exact match must find it and nothing else.

### before

`````markdown
# Guide

Paragraph one, which will be rewritten entirely.

Paragraph two, which survives this rewrite untouched.

Paragraph three, which will also be rewritten entirely.
`````

### after

`````markdown
# Manual

A completely different opening that shares no wording with what it replaced.

Paragraph two, which survives this rewrite untouched.

Another completely different closing, sharing nothing with its predecessor either.
`````

- before[2] => after[2]

## case: rewrite-bodies-not-headings

**why:** the skeleton survives and the flesh does not — the shape a re-import of a regenerated document usually has. Headings carry; bodies must not.

### before

`````markdown
## Concurrency

The old body of the concurrency section.

## Excision

The old body of the excision section.
`````

### after

`````markdown
## Concurrency

Replacement wording that shares nothing with what stood here.

## Excision

Also replaced, also sharing nothing with its predecessor.
`````

## case: rewrite-truncate

**why:** the tail of the document is dropped. Nothing that survives has changed, so any missed binding is pure loss.

### before

`````markdown
# Doc

Alpha paragraph that stays.

Beta paragraph that stays.

Gamma paragraph that goes.

Delta paragraph that goes.
`````

### after

`````markdown
# Doc

Alpha paragraph that stays.

Beta paragraph that stays.
`````

## case: rewrite-document-doubled

**why:** the document is appended to itself — the classic bad sync. Every block now has two equal-hash candidates.
**ambiguous:** the copies are byte-identical, so nothing can distinguish them; document order decides which occurrence continues the original.

### before

`````markdown
# Doc

Alpha paragraph.

Beta paragraph.
`````

### after

`````markdown
# Doc

Alpha paragraph.

Beta paragraph.

# Doc

Alpha paragraph.

Beta paragraph.
`````
